What do these numbers mean? ▼
Heart Rate (BPM) — Pulse rate extracted from subtle skin-colour variations caused by blood-volume changes (rPPG).

SpO₂ (%) — Oxygen saturation estimated via Red / Blue channel light-absorption ratio. Normal range: 95–100%.

HRV & Stress — RMSSD measures heart-rate variability. Higher HRV → Lower physiological stress.
